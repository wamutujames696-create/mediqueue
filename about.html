ABOUT 22 MEDIQUEUE

Better connected care. A healthier everyday.

22 MediQueue is a digital healthcare access and wellness platform concept designed to make the patient journey feel simpler, more informed and more human.

OUR PURPOSE

Less waiting.
More caring.

We believe people should spend less energy navigating a hospital visit and more time focusing on their wellbeing. MediQueue brings patients, healthcare teams and administrators into one organised digital experience.

Alongside queue and appointment tools, our public wellness guides make it easier to explore everyday nutrition, movement and healthy routines.

01

People first

Clear information and approachable tools should support every patient.

02

Better coordination

Queue visibility and appointment requests help care teams organise service.

03

Everyday wellbeing

Accessible education can encourage thoughtful food, movement and rest habits.
