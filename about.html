ABOUT 22 MEDIQUEUE

Built around better care.

22 MediQueue is designed to make hospital visits easier to navigate. Patients can discover departments, choose a doctor, request appointments and follow queue updates, while care teams and administrators coordinate service delivery.

People first

Clear information and simple steps help patients feel prepared and supported.

Connected care

Patients, doctors, staff and administrators use role-based workspaces.

Less friction

Queue visibility and appointment requests help make each visit easier to plan.
