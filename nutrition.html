FOOD FOR EVERYDAY WELLBEING

Build a balanced plate.

Try variety, balance and consistency rather than searching for one perfect food.

Vegetables & fruit

Choose different colours and types when available. Fresh, frozen or appropriately prepared options can all contribute.

Staples & whole grains

Matooke, sweet potatoes, posho, rice and other staples can provide energy. Vary choices and portions to suit your needs.

Protein foods

Beans, peas, groundnuts, eggs, fish, milk and lean meats can contribute protein and other nutrients.

Healthy fats & fluids

Use oils and nuts in sensible amounts and drink safe water regularly. Needs vary with activity, climate and health.

Simple meal ideas

  • Matooke with beans and a side of vegetables.
  • Posho with groundnut sauce and greens.
  • Sweet potato with eggs and fruit.
  • Rice with fish or beans and vegetables.
  • Plain yoghurt or milk with fruit, if tolerated.

These are ideas, not a prescribed diet. Adjust for allergies, budget, appetite and medical advice.

Food planning tips

  • Plan a few affordable meals before shopping.
  • Include a protein source in main meals.
  • Wash produce and prepare food safely.
  • Eat regularly if that suits your health needs.
  • Notice which foods personally trigger discomfort.
